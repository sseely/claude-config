# Research Sources

Start at Tier 1 (authoritative); descend only when the higher tier has
no answer. Declare confidence per this table when accuracy matters — the
tier backing a claim sets the ceiling on what may be declared:

| Level | Means | Typical backing |
|-------|-------|-----------------|
| **HIGH** | Verified against a source retrieved this run | Tier 1, or Tier 2 with the paper cited |
| **MEDIUM** | Single source, or strong training knowledge — state the caveat | One Tier 3 source, or an uncorroborated Tier 2 finding |
| **LOW** | Memory only, unverified — say so plainly | Tier 4 preprint, or Tier 5 with nothing under it |
| **UNKNOWN** | Cannot verify | Nothing retrievable — admit it rather than fabricate |

Full tier list and citation format: `docs/reference/research-sources.md`.
